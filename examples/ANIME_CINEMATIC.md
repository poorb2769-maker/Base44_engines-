# Anime Cinematic Demo - Naruto-Style\n\nShowcase the visual quality and storytelling capabilities with anime-style cinematics.\n\n## Vision\n\nCreate a cinematic experience inspired by Naruto Shippuden featuring:\n- Anime-style character models\n- Cinematic camera work\n- Naruto-inspired combat effects\n- Dialogue with branching choices\n- Japanese audio and subtitles\n- Cut scenes with transitions\n\n## Features\n\n### Visual Effects\n- **Bloom**: Anime glow effect on characters\n- **Motion Blur**: Dynamic camera movements\n- **Depth of Field**: Cinematic focus effects\n- **Film Grain**: Anime-style texture\n- **Particle Effects**:\n  - Ninja star trails\n  - Chakra auras\n  - Lightning effects\n  - Smoke clouds\n\n### Dialogue System\n- Branch-based story progression\n- Conditional choices (based on previous decisions)\n- Character emotions with animations\n- Japanese voice acting (optional)\n- Subtitle support\n\n### Combat Showcase\n- Chakra-based attack system\n- Multi-stage special moves\n- Combo system with auto-combos\n- Stage destruction\n- Environmental interaction\n\n### Characters\n1. **Naruto** - Hero character\n2. **Sasuke** - Rival character\n3. **Kakashi** - Mentor NPC\n4. **Itachi** - Boss character\n\n## Cinematic Sequences\n\n### Opening (0-30 seconds)\n- Establishing shot of Hidden Leaf Village\n- Pan to training ground\n- Character introduction\n\n### Main Story (30 seconds - 5 minutes)\n- Dialogue with Kakashi\n- Branching dialogue choices\n- Decision impacts story\n\n### Combat Sequence (5-10 minutes)\n- Epic battle with special effects\n- Combo showcases\n- Environmental destruction\n- Victory or defeat\n\n### Ending (10-12 minutes)\n- Character reflection\n- Dynamic camera movements\n- Credits roll\n\n## Implementation\n\n```typescript\nimport {\n  DialogueSystem,\n  CinematicSystem,\n  CombatSystem,\n  ParticleSystem,\n  PostProcessing\n} from '@base44/engine';\n\nclass AnimeDemo {\n  private dialogue: DialogueSystem;\n  private cinematics: CinematicSystem;\n  private particles: ParticleSystem;\n  private postFX: PostProcessing;\n\n  async init() {\n    // Setup cinematic effects\n    this.postFX.addBloomEffect(0.7, 0.75); // Anime glow\n    this.postFX.addFilmGrainEffect(0.3);\n    \n    // Start opening sequence\n    await this.cinematics.playSequence('opening');\n  }\n\n  playDialogueScene() {\n    this.dialogue.startDialogue('kakashi_greeting');\n  }\n}\n```\n\n## File Structure\n\n```\nexamples/anime-cinematic/\n├── src/\n│   ├── game.ts\n│   ├── characters/\n│   │
